# Day 2: Native American Culture

The people who came to North and South America via Beringia grew in number and formed civilizations with unique cultures. These are called **indigenous peoples** ("indigenous" means that something or someone is naturally from an area). 

In Mesoamerica (modern day Yucatan Peninsula in Mexico) were the Olmec, Aztec, and Mayan civilizations. The first Americans, the Olmecs, learned to raise vegetables such as corns and tomatoes and thrived in areas such as art and written language. The Mayans, known for their tiered pyramids, and the Aztecs, known for their well-organized society and strong military, existed in some form until being conquered by early Spanish explorers.

Farther south, the Incans had a complex society with a worshipped king, impressive architecture, and systems of roads.
