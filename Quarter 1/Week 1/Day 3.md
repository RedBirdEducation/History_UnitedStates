# Day 3: Native American Culture (Cont.)

Today, research foods Native Americans ate and make one (or more) to eat. Some examples include the following:
* Corn
* Cornbread
* Squash
* Pumpkin
* Salmon

Think about how difficult it would be to eat if you either had to grow or had to hunt all your food.
