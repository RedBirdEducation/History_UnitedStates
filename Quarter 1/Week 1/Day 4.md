# Day 4: Discovery of America

Native Americans were the first inhabitants in our land, but how did other people end up here?

Many historians believe that a Norse Viking named Leif Erickson likely was the first European to discover the **New World** (i.e., the Western Hemisphere, which was previously unknown to Europeans; the Americas). However, he and his crew decided not to stay in America permanently.

The first European to come to America and create a permanent colony was **Christopher Columbus**. Columbus sailed to parts of what is now Central America, the Dominican Republic, and Cuba. However, although he never stepped foot on what is now the United States, his "discovery" opened the door for other settlers to come to the New World over the next couple of centuries.
