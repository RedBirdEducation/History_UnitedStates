# Day 11: Plymouth

Thirteen years after the establishment of Jamestown, another English colony, **Plymouth**, was founded. 

To start our study of Plymouth, let's learn more about why those in the colony decided to immigrate to the New World. We'll also read the **Mayflower Compact**, the first governing document in the New World. Read the sections about "The Anglo-Powhatan Wars" and "The Rise of Slavery in the Chesapeake Bay Colonies" in [Section 3.3 of the textbook](https://openstax.org/books/us-history/pages/3-3-english-settlements-in-america)
