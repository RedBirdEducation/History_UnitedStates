# Day 20: French and Indian War

Yesterday, you learned how the Enlightenment prepared the minds of the colonists and how the First Great Awakening prepared the hearts of the colonists. Today, let's take a look at something else that prepared the colonists for the road ahead: the **French and Indian War**.
  
Start by reading section 4.5 of the textbook: [Section 4.5: Wars for Empire](https://openstax.org/books/us-history/pages/4-4-great-awakening-and-enlightenment)

The French and Indian War (named from a colonist's perspective of fighting against the French and their Indian allies) was also known as the Seven Years' War (named for the duration of the conflict which reached other parts of the globe). Three lasting effects resulted from the war:
1. French influence in the New World was largely diminished.
2. Many colonists gained military experience.
3. Britain became deeply in debt.

To learn more about the French and Indian War (and for a little more on The First Great Awakening), watch the following video: [The Seven Years War and the Great Awakening](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5vKGU3aEGss)
