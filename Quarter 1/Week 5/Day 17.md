# Day 17: Middle Colonies

Up to this point, you've learned mostly about Spanish and English colonization in the New World. Today, when looking at the Middle Colonies (New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware), we'll see that some of the thirteen original colonies that became the United States were established by other countries before being claimed by England. 

Read the following sections from the textbook: 
* ["Fur Trading in New Netherland"](https://openstax.org/books/us-history/pages/3-2-colonial-rivalries-dutch-and-french-colonial-ambitions)
* ["Charles II," "New York and New Jersey," "Pennsylvania"](https://openstax.org/books/us-history/pages/4-1-charles-ii-and-the-restoration-colonies) (Skip the section about the Carolinas for now)

One colony that the textbook does not discuss in much detail is Delaware, so watch the following video for a crash course on its history: [Delaware Is Tiny...So Why Does It Exist?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ACXwkQATgAk)
