# Day 6: First Permanent Settlement

The Conquistadores explored the New World, but they needed places to live if they were going to stay in the New World permanently. On behalf of Spain, the Conquistadores claimed areas of land and then established colonies. 

The Spanish had already established colonies elsewhere in the New World, but in 1565, **St. Augustine** in Florida was established as the first permanent settlement in what is now the United States.
