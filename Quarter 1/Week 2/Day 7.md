# Day 7: Beginning of English Exploration

Because Christopher Columbus sailed on behalf of Spain, it was the first country to start laying claim to the New World. Even though Spanish explorers like Hernando de Soto explored as far north as modern day Tennessee, most of the early Spanish colonies were farther south on the continent. 

However, other countries in Europe wanted to compete with Spain for supremacy in the New World. One country that would later play a major role in the development of our country was England.

Read the first three paragraphs under "English Exploration in [Section 2.3 of the textbook](https://openstax.org/books/us-history/pages/2-3-challenges-to-spains-supremacy) to learn about the early motives and efforts by England in colonizing the New World.
