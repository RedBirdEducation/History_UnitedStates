# Day 8: Why Did Europeans Come to the New World?

So far, we've learned about the Spanish coming to the New World, and we've started looking at early English colonists. Before we explore any further, let's think about why European settlers came to the New World. After all, didn't they have nice lives in Europe without having to cross an ocean and build a whole society from the ground up?

Historians highlight three main factors that drove Europeans to immigrate to the New World:
1. Gold - Some came to the New World hoping to get rich, whether by finding resources like farmland, gold, silver, or spices or by discovering a quicker way to sail to Asia for trade.
2. God - Many European Christians believed it was their calling to spread the Gospel to the Native Americans. Other European Christians suffered persecution for their beliefs and traveled to the New World to worship freely.
3. Glory - Some countries just wanted the glory and power of conquering and claiming more territory. On a personal level, some of the people exploring on behalf of these countries wanted to be recognized as national heroes or just to embark on an exciting adventure.
