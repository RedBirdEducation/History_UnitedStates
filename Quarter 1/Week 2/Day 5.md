# Day 5: Spanish Explorers

When Christopher Columbus discovered the New World, he did so on behalf of Spain. Therefore, the Spaniards were the first to colonize the New World. Specifically, the **Conquistadores** were Spanish explorers who came to conquer and claim the New World.

The first Conquistador to explore what is now the United States was **Hernando de Soto**. De Soto and his men explored what is now Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Alabama, Mississippi, Arkansas, Louisiana, and Texas. When he traveled to Arkansas and Texas, he became the first European to cross the Mississippi River.

Watch the below video for a quick overview of de Soto's exploration: </br>
[Exploration: Hernando de Soto](https://www.history.com/topics/exploration/videos?video=25)

Another early Conquistador who explored what would become the United States was Ponce de Leon. De Leon explored what is now Florida. While in Florida, he wondered why the Native Americans were so much taller and lived so much longer than the Europeans. De Leon reached the conclusion that there must have been a fountain of youth! Watch this video to learn more about Ponce de Leon and his search for the fountain: </br>
