# Day 16: Puritan Colonies

Today, you'll learn about the Puritan Colonies. Actually, you've already learned about the first of the Puritan Colonies: Plymouth Colony. The other Puritan Colonies are as follows:
* Massachusetts - the Massachusetts Bay Colony was established in 1630 by Puritans fleeing England and was merged with Plymouth Colony in 1691 to form the Massachusetts colony
* Rhode Island - established in 1636 by Roger Williams in an effort to offer true freedom of religion to its settlers
* Connecticut - established in 1636 by Thomas Hooker in an effort to expand voting rights of settlers
* New Hampshire - originally part of the Massachusetts Bay Colony, New Hampshire was declared a separate colony in 1679
