# Day 22: The War Begins

The protests in Boston grew more and more tense. Eventually, British troops (also known as **"redcoats"**) were ordered to arrest some of the rebel leaders and to capture the ammunition stored in Concord, Massachusetts. However, three men--Paul Revere, William Dawes, and Samuel Prescott--set off to warn the colonial militias that the British were coming. 

When the British arrived, the colonists awaited them, and the war broke out with the "shot heard 'round the world." The best way to learn about the ensuing battle(s) is to watch a re-enactment: [American Revolution, Battles of Lexington and Concord History Film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F1rLiizOyAk)
